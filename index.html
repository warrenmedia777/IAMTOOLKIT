"The reader is being read."

The reader is being read. Students are not simply reading information online. They are not simply getting answers to questions that they have. They are also being read by the very tools that they are using. Not only are they capturing their attention, but they are also capturing their ideas and creative inspiration. This framework teaches students to understand the context they're in and develop independent thinking.
